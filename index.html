Project 01 / Theatrical Scenic Design Independent · Annie Cheng · 2025

Blithe Spirit

By Noël Coward · Scenic Design

01 / Overview

Project Overview

This project is a scenic design for Noël Coward's play Blithe Spirit. The design explores the contrast between everyday domestic life and the supernatural presence of ghosts. Through architectural space, interior elements, and lighting atmosphere, the stage environment reflects both order and disruption within the story.

Central image
An elegant living room with sunlight streaming in, while the air remains filled with a sense of stagnation after conflict. Broken glass and a shattered vase become physical proof of Elvira's ghost. By the end, the spirits of Ruth and Elvira are still in the house.

02 / Design Rationale

Concept Development

My design originates from the absurd tension between the supernatural and ordinary domestic life in Blithe Spirit. The living room appears elegant and orderly, but subtle disruptions suggest the presence of ghosts.

Broken objects, shifting light, and spatial openness emphasize the unstable boundary between reality and the supernatural. Flickering practical lights combine with cold illumination to allow the atmosphere to change without transforming the room into an obviously haunted space.

Historical & Visual Research

I researched British domestic interiors from the 1940s to understand the visual culture of middle-class homes during the period. Late Art Deco furniture and postwar utility design influenced the arrangement, detailing, and atmosphere of the living room.

03 / Environment & Materials

Design Approach

The design reimagines the traditional enclosed living room as a more open and layered environment. Inspired by British country houses surrounded by lush vegetation, large windows and French doors visually connect the interior with the outside landscape.

This openness allows Elvira to move fluidly between interior and exterior space. The contrast between the natural landscape and domestic interior creates a clear separation while evoking an otherworldly sensation.

05 / From Drawing to Model

Design Process

The process moved from elevation studies and architectural details to small paper prototypes and a complete scale model. Repeated testing focused on the balance between symmetry, the openness of the French doors, and the layered relationship between the room and garden.

The final model preserves the elegance of a 1940s sitting room while leaving enough visual depth for light, movement, and supernatural interruption.

Project 02 / Immersive Media Unity 2022.3 · Meta Quest 3 · 2025

Virtual Space
Gundam Museum

An explorable archive of the Universal Century

Team Members: Spike Rao, Annie Cheng, Aliston Ma

01 / Overview

Project Description

The VR Universal Century Museum is an immersive virtual exhibition set immediately after the events of the One Year War of 0079 in the Mobile Suit Gundam timeline. Designed as a fully explorable museum for the Meta Quest 3, the project guides visitors through humanity's migration from Earth to the space colonies, the political tensions that arose, and the devastating conflict that reshaped the Universal Century.

The experience begins in a photo gallery with touch-activated narration. Visitors then enter a 3D model hall showcasing spacecraft, colony designs, and mobile suits, including the RX-78-2 Gundam and MS-06 Zaku II.

Historical narrative and speculative world-building meet through visual storytelling, spatial audio, and hands-on interaction, bringing the Universal Century to life for Gundam fans and newcomers alike.

04 / Design Rationale

Conceptual Statement

This project reimagines the rich lore of the Mobile Suit Gundam Universal Century as a tangible, explorable cultural institution. By situating the experience after the One Year War, the museum functions as both an archive of a fictional past and a speculative reflection on humanity's future in space.

The design emphasizes commemoration, education, and immersion. It provides context for the socio-political forces behind space colonization and the tragedies of interstellar conflict.

VR transforms passive observation into active engagement: visitors explore at their own pace, discover hidden narratives, and physically connect with artifacts. This interaction bridges historical museum conventions with the possibilities of immersive media.

07 / Production

Tools & Materials Used

We constructed the futuristic exhibition hall in Unity through 3D environment assembly and dynamic lighting. The project combines virtual reality and AI production methods to turn a fictional history into a walkable, responsive museum environment.

03 / Immersive EnvironmentUnity + Meta Quest 3 · 2026 · Group Work

Seasoned

A Chinese garden through the seasons

Space · Atmosphere · Discovery

01 / Project Overview

A garden,
gradually discovered.

Seasoned draws on a classical principle of Chinese garden design: a garden should never be seen all at once, but discovered gradually as the visitor moves through it.

This VR experience reimagines that idea as a walkable environment of pavilions, covered corridors, and moon gates. Spring blossoms, summer shade, autumn color, and winter snow transform the world around the visitor.

The project builds on my earlier Vectorworks landscape model, extending a static design into a real-time, immersive environment.

02 / Seasonal Environment

Spring

I designed Spring as a small town inspired by a traditional Chinese morning market. Shops, streets, and gathering spaces invite visitors to explore at their own pace.

*Annie ChengEnvironment layout · Lighting & skybox
Particle effects · Sound design

03 / Spatial Design

Waterways
& crossings.

Cross-shaped waterways divide the town into four areas. Small bridges reconnect the canal paths and market streets, creating a sequence of open views and more intimate spaces.

The layout encourages visitors to discover the environment through movement, with architecture and water shaping each new perspective.

04 / Atmosphere & Sound

A softer
season.

I created an AI-generated pink skybox and adjusted the lighting to give the scene a soft spring atmosphere. Falling petal particles suggest a breeze passing through the trees.

The sound design combines spring-inspired background music with flowing water around the canals, adding another layer to the experience of exploring the town.
